Definitions & method

  • Acquired: paid-ad lead (Meta UTM) whose company is a signed installer (status active); prospects/tests excluded. 221 leads matched by company + name, generic-word matches removed by hand.
  • Still active: signed installer not offboarded (is_active).
  • Credited month: when the ad lead arrived (not account create date).
  • Chargeable survey: stage Survey Done, Booked or Not Completed (cancelled / no-place / refunded excluded), from the slots table, priced at each installer's real rate.
  • Net profit: (revenue − ad spend) × 30% margin. Spend = Meta Ads API, 1 Jan – 5 Jun 2026.
