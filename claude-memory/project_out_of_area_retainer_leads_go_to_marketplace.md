A retainer client's homeowner outside their area who books a survey lands as an open marketplace slot (slots.status='available', placed_with_company_id null) for the old Green Tide marketplace installers to claim. That is the designed route, not a bug and not ours (Charlotte 2026-10-18). Hub figures count only surveys placed with the client.

**Why:** I flagged 26 of these as a problem on 10-18; Charlotte: "this isnt our issue, it just goes to be placed with our greentide old marketplace installers".
**How to apply:** don't raise unclaimed out-of-area retainer slots as an issue; just make sure client figures never count them. See [[feedback-client-answers-final-no-extra-problems]].
