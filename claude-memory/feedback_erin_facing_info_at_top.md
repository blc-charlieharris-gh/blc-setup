Anything Erin has to act on (e.g. which campaign a creative test launches into) goes where she reads first: the task pop-up's top summary box, the "Next:" line (taskWhereAt) and the Taskboard card. A small grey line in the brief is not enough.

**Why:** 10-25 I first put the campaign as "Test plan: ... on the AS03 campaign" in the brief; Charlotte: "this isn't clear enough, Erin won't see it". Second PR (#2427) added a purple "Launch into" box, campaign in the Next step and an "Into <campaign>" card line.

**How to apply:** when a task needs Erin to do something specific, show it in all three places in the first change. Related: [[feedback_whole_impact_before_any_change]].
