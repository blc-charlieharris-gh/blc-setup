When Charlotte asks to reframe marketing copy with feedback points:
- Keep the last version's headline and structure; add the new angles around it. She called my new headline "TERRIBLE" and said "don't remove what worked last time".
- A fact someone "told me" (dates, cuts, launches) is speculation unless confirmed: hedge it.
- "Partnered with X" does not mean X presents. Ask what role a partner plays before writing speaker cards.
- She works copy in her own ChatGPT ("use gpt"). There's no OpenAI access on the Mac: give her the original page text (or a brief file in TextEdit) to paste, then build exactly what she pastes back on top of the original.

**Why:** 27 Oct 2026 webinar rewrite went through three wrong versions before she used ChatGPT copy.
