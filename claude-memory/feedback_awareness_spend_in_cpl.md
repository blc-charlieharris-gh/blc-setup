Charlotte 2026-10-09, rejecting batch A's calculator change (which took Awareness spend out of InstallrHub's CPL, £55 → £25): "the awareness campaigns are linked to the lead campaigns, they fuel what the lead campaigns' traffic is, so it needs to be factored into the cost per lead."

**Why:** TOF/MOF warm up the audience the lead campaigns convert, so a CPL without them understates the real cost.
**How to apply:**
- CPL = (Leads + Awareness spend) ÷ leads.
- Sign-ups (magnet) keep their own cost per sign-up / per booked call.
- Awareness stays out of LISTS where it doesn't belong: Creative Testing, the Dashboard campaign list, tagging, and the too-many-ads check. It is never out of the money.
- Check any change touching spend or CPL against this rule before building.
