2026-10-07, Charlotte on The Elect Group ads: say "grant" (NOT discount) and always put "up to" before the grant amount, in every body, headline, description, form question and image. I misread "we say discount instead of grant" the wrong way round once; the rule is grant.

**Why:** compliance/accuracy on the Boiler Upgrade Scheme amount; the client (Dave Walkden at Elect) paused all ads the same day over the wording.
**How to apply:** before launching or editing any heat pump grant ad, scan copy, the lead form question text and every design slot for "discount" or a £ amount without "up to". Forms can't be edited once published: make a new form, then the Hub needs a lead route for the new form id (meta_lead_form_routes) or leads never reach the client. If an instruction's direction is ambiguous ("X instead of Y"), confirm before changing live ads. Related: [[feedback_meta_lead_form_api_limits]]
