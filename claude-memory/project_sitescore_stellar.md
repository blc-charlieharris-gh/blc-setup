**Verified findings (evidence-cited):**
1. **DEAD DEADLINE, the headline.** Site says ECO4 ends "March 2026" / "31 March 2026" / "effective until March 31, 2026" in 3 places incl. an Elementor counter titled "ECO4 GRANTS AVAILABLE UNTIL March 2026". Today = 16 Jul 2026, so the advertised deadline passed ~4 months ago: a homeowner concludes the scheme is dead and leaves. **ECO4 was actually extended to 31 Dec 2026** (gov.uk consultation response), so they are killing live demand for a scheme that still has ~5 months to run.
2. **Strategic ceiling.** ECO4 ends 31 Dec 2026 with **no ECO5 / no successor supplier obligation**; successor is the grant-funded Warm Homes Plan (Warm Homes: Local Grant, council-run). **CAUTION (Charlotte, 2026-07-16): do NOT infer they can still sell ECO4.** Scheme window open ≠ that installer still has supplier funding, which is invisible from outside. The defensible line is "the date is wrong either way": funding live → turning away real jobs; funding spent → advertising what they can't supply. See [[feedback_sitescore_dont_assume_the_service]].
2b. **Positioning by default.** Homepage says "boiler" **68x** vs "solar" **4x** and "heat pump" **5x**; the services list (incl. solar + ASHP) sits **90-92% down** a 21,363px page. H1 "Free Boiler Grants", title "Eco4 Boiler Grant | Free Boiler". Company is called Stellar *Insulation* and says insulation 17x. Root cause = "the site froze, and now it is choosing your customers for you". The audit ASKS which homeowner they want (Stage 0 = a 20-min conversation); it does not prescribe a service.
3. **Trust under-use (positive framing).** 4.6/5 from 77 Trustpilot reviews and an active TrustMark licence exist, and **neither appears on the site**. Zero reviews, zero ratings, zero star widgets, no TrustMark/Gas Safe/NICEIC numbers. Badges are **images with alt text only**, no numbers, no links, so unverifiable by a homeowner and unreadable by Google/AI.
4. **Factually wrong eligibility cards.** "Child Benefit" card body describes **Universal Credit**; "Universal Credit" card body describes **Income Support**. On the eligibility section, the single most important content on an ECO4 site. "Income-based Jobseeker's Allowance" body has a grammar break (missing "claiming").
5. **AI-slop copy.** 9 near-identical benefit blocks of filler ("beacon of positive change", "stands as a testament to progress and inclusivity"). FAQPage schema includes **"How does the ECO4 Grant contribute to sustainable transportation initiatives?"** answering about EVs and public transport infrastructure, on a boiler site.
6. **Self-contradiction.** "We are an end-to-end company, meaning there is no third party involved" vs the 3-step section: "we will connect you with an approved boiler installer. They will contact you".
7. **Two front doors + the header email goes to the OLD one (Fix 07, PR #31).** `stellarinsulation.com` is a **separate, older GoDaddy WordPress site** ("Stellar Insulation at your service!", orange #eb6737), live and unconnected to the Elementor `.co.uk`. The **envelope icon in the header of every page** links `mailto:info@stellarinsulation.com` (the `<span class="tel">` label is CSS-hidden, hence absent from innerText, but the link is live and clickable, 41x51px); the `.co.uk` address only appears in the contact section 96% down. **DNS proves they're different mailboxes on different platforms:** `.co.uk` MX = `aspmx.l.google.com` (Google Workspace), `.com` MX = `stellarinsulation-com.mail.protection.outlook.com` (Microsoft 365); A records `35.214.110.2` (SiteGround) vs `160.153.0.120` (GoDaddy). The `.com` accepts mail so nothing bounces = enquiries may be landing silently in a retired site's inbox. Report states we can't see whether it's monitored and tells them to check.
8. **"Age Concern Grant"** used in copy + the about-us page title ("Free Boiler Replacement for Pensioner | over 60's | Age concern Grant"). Age Concern merged into Age UK in 2009; there is no Age Concern boiler grant. Charity-brand misuse = real compliance risk for an ECO lead-gen site.
9. No social links anywhere on the site (only the Meta pixel), despite an FB page + LinkedIn existing.
