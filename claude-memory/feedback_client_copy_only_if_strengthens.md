Charlotte 2026-10-29 (Gas Worx case study): "only add or change if it strengthens argument". I proposed correcting 129 to 132 m² and adding a very low heat loss figure; she said no. The low heat loss would weaken it (a reader thinks "of course, a new build").

**Why:** client copy is persuasion, signed off by the client; small corrections and weakening facts cost a round with them for no gain.
**How to apply:** before suggesting a copy change on a client site, ask "does this make the case stronger?" If not, leave it, unless it's legally wrong.
