Charlotte (2026-11-01): "its just so unclear what step im up to and what to do next". Approved flow (mock-up https://claude.ai/artifact/Mw6TYoZu3stcYdjYDy7jP2):
- One box at the top of the job: the step you're on, ONE next thing, what waits on the client.
- 6 Build and check is the only step with the kit, the zip upload and the audit, every round. 7 Ask the client. They loop until approval; changes touching earlier steps (site map, words) are flagged and fixed in 6, never "go back". 8 Hand over, 9 blog. Ask for a review lives on the client card.
- One button for the AI: Download the kit (holds this round's list, copies the paste line). The upload button is called "Upload zip", never "Save your AI's zip".
- Client-only items are listed apart from red fixes, never as a fix.

**Why:** catching sites up showed the old 12-step page made her read four places to work out what to do.
**How to apply:** any new website-job feature fits this loop and the top box; no new step, no second copy button, no extra explanations. Related: [[feedback_no_parallel_copies_of_one_job]].
