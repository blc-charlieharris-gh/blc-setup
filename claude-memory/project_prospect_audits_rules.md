Charlotte 2026-10-11, on the Crew prospect audits (ProspectAudits, crew audit B6.3 moves it to Crew > Audits): keep it, "handy as long as we can merge into a client if sold".
- Only PROSPECTS are added there (site, social or search audit). Clients always come from the InstallrHub app, never added in Crew.
- 30 days after a prospect's audit: raise "Did this close?" (Actions card). No -> archive the prospect. Yes -> sync/merge into the client record.
- Keep matching prospect company names to client names (exact and similar), so when a client signs up later (e.g. 3 months) the Hub asks "Did they get a site audit? Merge?".

Confirmed 10-12 (B6 preview): Crew > Audits lists prospects only, with an "Add prospect" button. A prospect can hold several audits, one per kind (SiteScore now; Search score and Social score coming). On merge (Hub suggests by similar name, she confirms with one click, never automatic) the audits leave Crew > Audits and show on the client card's Crew tab beside their jobs (option A; per-client Workspace pages still go). A client's own bought site audit stays with its job.

**Why:** prospect audits warm up sales; the work and score should follow them onto the client record if they buy.
**How to apply:** build into crew audit B6 (Crew > Audits) after confirming details with her; one merge path, no second client list. Related: [[project_hub_page_audit]], [[project_client_area_plan]].
