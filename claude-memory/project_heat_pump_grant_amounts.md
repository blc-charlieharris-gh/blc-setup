Charlotte 2026-10-13 ("I'm always having to correct you"): the heat pump grant (Boiler Upgrade Scheme, England and Wales) is **up to £9,000 for homes currently heated by oil or LPG, until March 2027**; otherwise up to £7,500. Always say "grant" and "up to £X", never "discount" (see [[elect-grant-wording-rule]]). Ads like "not long left for oil homeowners to get the increased 9k grant" rely on it.

**Why:** Claude kept defaulting to £7,500 only, and copy (Crew AI writer, ads) came out without the stronger oil/LPG offer.
**How to apply:** any copy, prompt or check that mentions the heat pump grant uses these two figures and the March 2027 end date; in the Hub the AI writer's prompt reads them from src/lib/grantFacts.js (one place). After March 2027, ask Charlotte what replaces it.
