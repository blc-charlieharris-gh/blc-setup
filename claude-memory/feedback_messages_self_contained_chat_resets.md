Charlotte (2026-10-06): "when you update with code I lose the chat, come back and you're just asking a question about something I've had no context or explanation on."

**Why:** her editor view of the conversation resets/collapses during long build turns, so earlier explanations vanish from what she sees. A bare "A or B?" is meaningless to her.

**How to apply:** every message that asks her to decide or do something restates, in plain words, what it's about and why (1-3 sentences) right there, even if explained before. Keep a short "where we are" list (done / waiting on you / building) at the end of long turns. Answer every point she raised; if one was missed (e.g. her "should this be a broadcast?" question), answer it explicitly.
