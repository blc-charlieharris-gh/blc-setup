Verified 2026-08-03 against MCS / government data (reported 11 Jun 2026, corroborated by PHAM News, Cooling Post, pv magazine).

**The split that matters:**
- **Retrofit** (existing home, homeowner decides, can be advertised to): Q1 2026 = **10,693 installs, down 22% year-on-year** and 18% on the previous quarter. Jan to Jul 2026 ≈ 27,500, also **-22%**.
- **New build** (developer fits them, no homeowner to market to): **up 38%** over the same period. Heat pumps' share of new-build sales went 55% → 61% in one quarter.

**Why it matters:** Charlotte confirmed we do NOT target new-build developers. Every survey InstallrHub sells is a retrofit homeowner, so the contracting half IS the addressable market. The market overall is fine; the part we sell into is not.

**Don't be fooled by the headline numbers.** "125,037 units in 2025, +27%" and "8,000-10,000 monthly in early 2026" are TOTALS including new build. Retrofit alone runs ~3,900/month and is falling. Two reports contradicted each other on this before the split was understood.

**Causes named:** Energy Company Obligation closing, and slow deployment through the Warm Homes: Social Housing Fund. Both policy rather than demand, so it can reverse.

**Perspective:** retrofit is still more than double Q1 2021 (4,607). This is a pullback from a subsidy-driven peak, not a collapse.
