On 2026-10-05 Charlotte said "i feel like you're telling me two things all the time" after hearing both "the funnel key isn't needed any more" and "GHL still sends it, don't remove it yet", and "GHL workflow not needed" vs "the GHL workflow must stay on".

**Why:** both halves were true but sounded contradictory; it cost trust and time on a busy day.
**How to apply:** lead with one sentence that holds both facts ("The Hub doesn't need it, but GHL still sends it, so it goes once those GHL steps are off"). Name which of two similar things you mean every time (e.g. "the funnel key webhook" vs "the form pass-through workflow"). Related: [[feedback-charlotte-plain-explanations]].
