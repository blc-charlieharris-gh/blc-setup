**Why:** my first HHE draft said "no offer" on a hero showing 0% VAT and finance, "never reach" when /quote/ showed reviews, and buried her in study research when she asked for a check. Then I over-corrected by chasing one menu link through four scripts.

**How to apply:** findings = what we saw + what it costs them, Fix = one-line result. Read the whole section and credit what's there. Hidden is not missing. Couldn't see it = leave it out, don't keep digging. Answers to her stay short. The rule lives in marketing-agent `.claude/docs/crew-audit-brain.md` section 3a, which goes into every kit.
