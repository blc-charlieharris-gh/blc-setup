Boiler Upgrade Scheme heat pump grant: £7,500 standard, £9,000 when the heat pump replaces an oil or LPG system, available "till March" (Charlotte, 2026-09-25). SWH's heat pump page quotes both figures and that is correct.

**Why:** on 2026-09-25 I told Charlotte the scheme was a flat £7,500 and suggested SWH confirm the £9,000 figure. She corrected me.

**How to apply:** don't flag £9,000 oil/LPG copy on client sites as an error. After March, check whether the uplift has ended, and if it has, the sites quoting £9,000 (SWH services-renewable-heating-cooling at least) need updating. Related: [[project-core-electrics-site]].
