Charlotte, 2026-10-06: "it's all part of greentide, greentide is our pps and ppl ad account." Marketplace / pay per lead area ad sets (AS01 etc., e.g. "GT | Solar | North West | Finance | AS01" feeding Kcglinks) run in the Green Tide ad account and their spend/leads count as Green Tide's. Do NOT split them out of Green Tide's numbers (the handoff's "area ad sets out of Green Tide numbers" step was dropped).

How the marketplace runs (Charlotte 10-06): every lead goes to Green Tide's GHL, and the call centre books it. An AS code links to one OR SEVERAL installers who share that area and tech. Its surveys are shared among them (InstallrHub splits the ad set's spend by surveys each got). Ad sets with no code are offered on the marketplace. Pay per lead skips the call centre. Meta's test tool can't attach an ad, so for a real end-to-end AS test, submit the form from the live ad's preview.

**Why:** Green Tide is the house account that sells surveys/leads to installers, not one client among many.
**How to apply:** per-installer views can show what an AS code fed them, but Green Tide totals keep everything. Related: [[project_greentide]], [[reference_greentide_meta_accounts]]
