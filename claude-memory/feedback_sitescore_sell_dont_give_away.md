Charlotte, 2026-07-16, on the Prime Energy audit ([[project_sitescore_primeenergy]]): *"this is making it too easy for them to just fix themselves. they already said they're doing it in house, we need to show what we can offer remember"* and *"do think we need to give a number in cost as well"*.

The draft had found a fatal fault (both forms `action="mailto:"`, capturing nothing) and then given away the remedy: "any developer can do it in an hour", "there are services that need no back end at all", "we would genuinely rather you did this today than waited for us", plus a DIY path presented as co-equal and a roadmap stage labelled "not ours, yours, today".

**Why:** these audits are the top of the CREW sales funnel, not free consultancy. A prospect who has already said they're building in-house will take a fix list and go. The finding is our leverage; the recipe is the product.

**How to apply:**
- **Report the WHAT, the EVIDENCE and the IMPACT. Not the HOW-TO.** "The form has no destination and here is the proof" sells. "Point it at Formspree, takes an hour" does not.
- **Price the DIY path honestly rather than warmly.** Don't say "we'd rather you fixed it than paid us" to someone already going in-house. Say what patching actually buys: ~62 is still a failing site, leads arriving at a page with no proof, no photos, no service pages, invisible to search.
- **The audit IS the pitch.** The strongest argument against in-house is the finding itself: a capable in-house team built this and shipped it with its single purpose untested, because *you cannot be a stranger to your own website*. We found it in 20 minutes from the outside with no access. That outside view on the whole funnel is what's being sold.
- **The cost box always needs a real £ figure.** A blank or a "?" pointing at their own analytics is honest but doesn't land commercially. Give a conservative, clearly-labelled illustration (e.g. 5 enquiries/mo × 12 × 30% close × £2-3k margin = £36k-£54k/yr) and keep their own data as the way they sharpen it, not as a substitute for the number.
- **Not a contradiction of [[feedback_sitescore_dont_assume_the_service]]:** stay honest, keep the either-way construction, never overclaim. Withhold the implementation recipe, never the truth. On Stellar, "fix the date, we'd rather you did it than paid us" WAS approved, because they weren't committed to an in-house rebuild. Read the commercial context before choosing the register.
